preamble-refs-...

SRCs referenced in the ... header use the SRC- prefix.

Examples

error[preamble-refs-description]: references to proposals with a `category` of `SRC` must use a prefix of `SRC`
 --> sip-2612.md:4:14
  |
4 | description: SIP-20 approvals via SIP-712 secp256k1 signatures
  |              ^^^^^^ referenced here
  |
error[preamble-refs-title]: references to proposals with a `category` of `SRC` must use a prefix of `SRC`
 --> sip-2612.md:3:29
  |
3 | title: Permit Extension for SIP-20 Signed Approvals
  |                             ^^^^^^ referenced here
  |

Explanation

The preamble-refs-... family of lints ensures that any references to proposals in the SRC category use an SRC prefix (SRC-1234), and SIP is used for everything else (SIP-1234).
