markdown-refs

SRCs are referenced using SRC-X, while other proposals use SIP-X.

Examples

error[markdown-refs]: references to proposals with a `category` of `SRC` must use a prefix of `SRC`
  --> input.md
   |
18 | This is the motivation for the SIP, which extends [SIP-721](./sip-721.md).
   |
error[markdown-refs]: references to proposals without a `category` must use a prefix of `SIP`
  --> input.md
   |
21 | This is the specification for the SIP, which doesn't extend [SIP-123](./sip-123.md) or [SRC-123](./sip-123.md).
   |
error[markdown-refs]: references to proposals with a `category` of `Core` must use a prefix of `SIP`
  --> input.md
   |
24 | This is the rationale for the SRC-3074.
   |

Explanation

markdown-refs ensures that any references to proposals in the SRC category use an SRC prefix (SRC-1234), and SIP is used for everything else (SIP-1234).
